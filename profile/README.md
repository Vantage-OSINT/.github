# Vantage

**Intelligence, connected.**

Vantage is an OSINT platform bringing people search, breach intelligence, court records, vehicle intelligence, online presence discovery, cryptocurrency investigations, and more into one unified workspace.

Built to help investigators connect identifiers, explore digital footprints, and turn fragmented data into structured intelligence.

**[Website](https://vsint.cc)** · **[Discord](https://discord.gg/CAdkxq3GKZ)**

### Intelligence, across multiple domains

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **People Intelligence** | Search people and available public records across the United States and Canada. |
| **Breach Intelligence** | Investigate known data breaches and exposed account information. |
| **Court Records** | Search available legal records and court-related information. |
| **Vehicle Intelligence** | Research license plates, identify associated VINs where supported, and explore vehicle specifications. |
| **Online Presence** | Discover publicly reported registrations, profiles, and services associated with email addresses, phone numbers, and usernames. |
| **Network Intelligence** | Investigate IP addresses, domains, DNS, TLS, and related network information. |
| **Crypto Intelligence** | Explore public blockchain addresses, transactions, token transfers, and on-chain activity. |
| **Company Intelligence** | Research businesses, corporate registrations, and organizational identifiers. |

### More than isolated searches

Vantage is designed around connected investigations, not just individual lookups.

- **Cross-referencing** — Follow identifiers discovered across different intelligence sources.
- **Relationship graphs** — Visualize connections using recorded search inputs and returned data.
- **Investigation history** — Revisit saved results, bookmarks, and linked reports.
- **Structured exports** — Preserve findings and source context for further analysis.
- **Unified workspace** — Access multiple intelligence capabilities through one interface.

### Built for intelligence work

Vantage serves OSINT researchers, security professionals, investigators, and analysts who need a more connected way to explore information across multiple sources.

Our focus is on bringing disparate data together while preserving source context and keeping reported information distinct from verified conclusions.
